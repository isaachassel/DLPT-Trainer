# Study audio provenance

These nine synthetic Russian recordings were generated during a paid Starter
subscription using generally available Eleven v3 and the owner's previously
approved Jessica/Eric premade-voice method. They contain original fictional
study dialogues, not official DLPT recordings or human speakers from the course.

`../audio.json` binds each MP3 to its script fingerprint, audio checksum,
creation-time plan, voices, model, date and reviewed provider terms. Standard
paid-plan commercial rights and no mandatory provider title attribution were
recorded on November 4, 2026, subject to applicable terms and input/voice rights.
The code's MIT license does not replace those terms. Standing method approval
does not claim individual human pronunciation or linguistic review.
