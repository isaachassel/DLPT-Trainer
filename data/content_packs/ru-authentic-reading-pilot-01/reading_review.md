### Passage 1 — 2017-06-09

Read this historical news report. Answer only from the passage, as of its publication date.

**[p1]** В День России, 12 июня, в честь праздника Музей современной истории страны будет работать бесплатно. Для посетителей организованы игры на знание отечественной географии.

**[p2]** В этот день у посетителей будет возможность попробовать себя в соревновании на знание географии родной страны. Желающим будет предложено собрать спилс-карту России, представляющую собой набор магнитных элементов в форме российских регионов, которые собираются на специальной магнитной доске в карту Российской Федерации. Соревнование команд на знание федеративного устройства страны начнется в 14.00.

**[p3]** День России — хороший повод для кого-то вспомнить, а для кого-то лучше узнать события недавнего прошлого. Только в Музее современной истории России находится экспозиция, охватывающая последние 30 лет нашей истории. Она рассказывает о непростом, насыщенном событиями пути, который страна прошла с 1985 года.

**1.1. Why will admission to the museum be free on June 12?**

- **A.** To mark the opening of a new exhibition.
- **B.** To celebrate a national holiday.
- **C.** To reward the winners of the geography competition.
- **D.** To commemorate the founding of the museum in 1985.

**1.2. What will participants in the geography activity be asked to do?**

- **A.** Arrange historical events in chronological order.
- **B.** Locate museum buildings on a city map.
- **C.** Draw regional boundaries on a blank board.
- **D.** Assemble a map using pieces shaped like regions.

**1.3. What period is the exhibition described in the final paragraph concerned with?**

- **A.** The country’s recent history beginning in 1985.
- **B.** The museum’s development over the preceding thirty years.
- **C.** The history of the national holiday since its creation.
- **D.** Changes in regional boundaries over several centuries.

## Review rubric

1. Verify every key from the excerpt alone; no world knowledge or missing article paragraphs should be necessary.
2. Confirm exactly one best answer. Reject an item if a second choice is defensible under an ordinary reading.
3. Preserve attribution, tense, negation, quantities, and qualifiers such as может, примерно, пока, and или чаще.
4. Distractors should reflect a plausible misunderstanding: wrong referent, wrong relationship, past versus planned, overstated certainty, or unrelated source detail.
5. Check that the correct answer is not consistently longer or more specific. Avoid grammatical clues and all/none-of-the-above choices.
6. Keep English stems and answers straightforward. Difficulty should come from Russian comprehension.
7. Ask inference questions only when the excerpt supports a narrow, defensible inference. Do not convert absence of information into a fact.
8. Distinguish the reporter’s voice, quoted views, and verified events. Do not endorse a source’s assertion merely by testing comprehension of it.
9. Review authentic source errors without silently correcting the passage. Replace a selection if its errors interfere with assessment.
10. Record reviewer, date, revisions, intended level, and approval. Pilot the items with learners before using performance to make level claims.

## What this pilot can and cannot establish

It can test the content-import flow and provide reviewed practice items. It cannot establish an ILR level from 18 uncalibrated items, cover the full DLPT domain, or supply an independent estimate of question difficulty. Two selections concern waste management, so the pilot is not a balanced domain blueprint. The 2008–2021 dates reflect reusable archived reporting rather than a current-news survey.

For a later listening pack, use original recorded speech with precise timestamps and documented reuse terms. Keep authentic source audio distinct from synthetic ElevenLabs practice audio. No audio or ElevenLabs account is needed for this reading pack.
